Here is the exact breakdown of the 2nd video’s mechanics:

1. The "Hero" Hero: The Spin-and-Scale Logic
The main salad bowl isn't just sitting there; it is the anchor of the entire UI.

Entrance: On page load or slide change, the main image (PNG) must rotate 360 degrees while simultaneously scaling from 0.5 to 1.2 and settling at 1.0.

The "Orbit" Carousel: When the user clicks "Next" or a thumbnail:

The current bowl must spin out (rotate + fade + move off-canvas).

The new bowl must spin in from the opposite side with a high-velocity rotation that eases into a stop.

CSS/JS Keyword: cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) (This gives that "snap" or "bounce" at the end of the spin).

2. The "Debris" Layer (Floating Ingredients)
This is what creates the "vibe." There are 4–6 small PNGs (leaves, tomatoes, etc.) scattered around the main product.

Independent Physics: Each small image needs its own animation-duration and float-path.

The Trick: They should not move in a straight line. Use a keyframe animation that combines translateY and rotate (e.g., rotating 15 degrees back and forth) to make them look like they are suspended in water or air.

Z-Index Layering:

Layer 1: Background (Text/Shapes).

Layer 2: Main Product (The Bowl).

Layer 3: Foreground "Debris" (Leaves that slightly overlap the bowl).

3. Typography & UI Layout
The reference uses a Minimalist High-Contrast Grid.

The Header: Sticky, transparent background, with thin-border buttons.

Content Alignment: The text is strictly left-aligned while the product is centered or slightly right-weighted. This creates "active white space."

Staggered Reveals: When scrolling to a new section, the text doesn't just appear; it should "slide up" 20px while fading in, with a 0.1s delay between the heading, the subtext, and the button.

4. Interactive Card Grid (The Menu)
Further down the video, the menu cards are clean white with a subtle shadow.

Hover State: The bowl on the card should scale up by 10% and rotate slightly (5–10 degrees) when the card is hovered. The card itself should lift slightly using a box-shadow transition.

The "Add to Cart" Button: Notice it’s a high-contrast green button that changes color or expands on hover.

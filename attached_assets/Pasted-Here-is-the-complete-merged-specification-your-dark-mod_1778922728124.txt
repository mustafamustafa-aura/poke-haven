## 🔄 THE MAIN ANIMATION — Orbiting Carousel

### Dashed Arc Path
- SVG curved dashed line running along the right half of the hero
- Color: `#F26522` at **35% opacity** — visible but subtle, feels premium against the dark background
- Dash pattern: `stroke-dasharray: 6 8`

### Orbiting Thumbnail Circles
- 4–5 circular thumbnail images traveling continuously along the arc
- Each thumbnail: round crop, **border: 2px solid `#1A2432`**, subtle box-shadow `0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`
- The **active thumbnail** (at the top of the arc): border switches to **`#F26522`** 2px, slightly larger scale (~1.15x)
- Movement: smooth, infinite, slow loop upward along the arc path — ~5s per full cycle
- Thumbnails fade in from the bottom of the arc and fade out as they exit the top

### Main Hero Food Image
- Displayed in a large circle on the right, clipped with `border-radius: 50%` or a circular mask
- Behind it: the subtle radial glow (`#1A2432` → `#0E1621`)
- **Crossfades** smoothly (opacity transition ~600ms) when the active thumbnail changes
- No hard border on the main image circle — the glow handles the separation from background

### Floating Leaves
- Color: **`#59B259` Leaf Green** — these now feel intentional and on-brand instead of decorative
- Each leaf: slightly transparent (~80% opacity), varying sizes (20px–55px)
- 8–10 leaves scattered across the right 60% of the screen, a few crossing into the left
- Each leaf has its own CSS keyframe: slow translate (drifting ~30–60px in one direction) + slow rotate (±20°), duration 6s–10s, `animation-timing-function: ease-in-out`, `animation-direction: alternate`, `infinite`
- No two leaves share the same timing — stagger start delays from 0s to –8s

### Quantity Counter
- Number ticks up (01 → 02 → 03) in sync with each carousel change
- Animation: short vertical slide — old number slides up and out, new number slides up and in (like a slot reel) — 200ms, ease-in-out
- Text color: `#FFFFFF`

## 📜 SECTION 2 — "Fuel Your Body" Second Hero

### Background
- Still **Deep Navy `#0E1621`** — seamless continuation from section 1, no visible divider

### Scroll Trigger
- As user scrolls down, this section's content **fades in + slides up 30px into position**
- `IntersectionObserver` with threshold 0.2 — triggers once element is 20% in view
- Duration: 600ms, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` easing

### Content
- Heading: **"Fuel Your Body with"** in `#FFFFFF`, then **"Nutrient-Rich"** in `#F26522`, then **"Bowls"** back in `#FFFFFF`
- Two body paragraphs: `#A0AEC0`
- **"Order Now"** button here: **Leaf Green `#59B259`** background, white text, pill shape — gives visual variety from section 1's orange button. Hover: `#48BB78` (slightly brighter green)
- Right side: food image in same circular frame style as section 1

### Floating Leaves
- Continue from section 1 — they drift across the boundary between sections seamlessly
- In this section they spread wider across the full width

## 📜 SECTION 4 — "Our Popular Menu"

### Background
- **`#0E1621`** deep navy, same as the rest of the site — fully consistent dark theme

### Heading
- **"Our"** `#FFFFFF` + **"Popular"** `#F26522` + **"Menu"** `#FFFFFF` — bold, center-aligned, large
- Subtext: `#A0AEC0`, centered, small, 1–2 lines

### Filter Tab Bar
- Pill-shaped tab row, centered
- Default tab: background `#1A2432`, text `#A0AEC0`, border `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`
- **Active tab**: background `#F26522`, text `#FFFFFF`, no border
- Hover state: border becomes `#F26522` at 50% opacity

### Product Cards (4 in a row)
Each card:
- Background: **`#1A2432`** surface color
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` default → `1px solid #F26522` on hover
- Border radius: `16px`
- Box shadow: `0 8px 32px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`
- **Hover effect**: card lifts — `transform: translateY(-6px)`, shadow deepens, border becomes orange
- Transition: `all 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`

Card internals:
- **Food image**: perfectly circular, centered at top of card, ~100px diameter, sits slightly overlapping the card top edge (negative margin-top effect)
- **Product name**: `#FFFFFF`, bold, ~15px
- **Star rating**: orange stars `#F26522`, rating number `#A0AEC0`
- **Description**: `#A0AEC0`, ~12px, 2 lines
- **Price**: `#FFFFFF`, bold, left-aligned
- **"Add to cart" button**: small pill, background `#59B259` (Leaf Green), white text + small cart icon — on hover: `#48BB78`

### Card Entry Animation (STAGGERED SCROLL TRIGGER)
- All 4 cards start invisible (`opacity: 0`, `transform: translateY(40px)`)
- When section enters viewport (IntersectionObserver), cards animate in one by one:
  - Card 1: delay 0ms
  - Card 2: delay 120ms
  - Card 3: delay 240ms
  - Card 4: delay 360ms
- Each card: `opacity 0→1` + `translateY 40px→0`, duration 500ms, ease-out

### "Explore More" Button
- Centered below cards
- Background: `#F26522`, white text, pill shape, medium padding
- Hover: slight scale `1.03` + brightness increase
- This alternates from the green button in section 2 — intentional orange/green rhythm across sections
